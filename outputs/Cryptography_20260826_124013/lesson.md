# Lesson: Introduction to Cryptography

## What is Cryptography?

Cryptography (pronounced "krip-tuh-gra-fee") is the science of protecting information by transforming it into a secure format. This means that only the people who are supposed to see the information can read it. Imagine writing a secret message that only your friend can understand. That’s what cryptography does!

### Why Does Cryptography Matter?

In our everyday lives, we share a lot of personal information, like passwords for online accounts, bank information, and messages. If someone could read this information without permission, it could lead to problems like identity theft or financial loss. Cryptography helps keep this information safe.

## How Does Cryptography Work?

Cryptography works by using two main processes: **encryption** and **decryption**.

### 1. Encryption

**Encryption** is the process of converting readable information (called plaintext) into a secret format (called ciphertext). This makes the information unreadable to anyone who doesn’t have the key to unlock it.

**Example of Encryption:**

Let’s say you want to send your friend a secret message: "HELLO".

Using a simple encryption method, you could change each letter to a different letter. For example:

- H → K
- E → G
- L → N
- O → Q

So, "HELLO" becomes "KGNNQ". Now, if someone sees "KGNNQ", they won't understand it without knowing the rules you used to change the letters.

### 2. Decryption

**Decryption** is the opposite of encryption. It is the process of converting the secret format (ciphertext) back into readable information (plaintext).

**Example of Decryption:**

Using the same example, if your friend receives "KGNNQ", they can use the same rules to convert it back:

- K → H
- G → E
- N → L
- Q → O

So, "KGNNQ" becomes "HELLO" again.

### How is Cryptography Used?

Cryptography is used in many areas, such as:

- **Online Shopping:** When you buy something online, your credit card information is encrypted to keep it safe.
- **Messaging Apps:** Apps like WhatsApp use encryption so that only you and the person you are chatting with can read the messages.
- **Secure Websites:** When you see a padlock symbol in your browser, it means the website uses encryption to protect your information.

## Summary

- **Cryptography** is about keeping information safe by making it unreadable to anyone who shouldn't see it.
- **Encryption** turns readable information into a secret format.
- **Decryption** turns the secret format back into readable information.
- Cryptography is important for protecting personal information online.

Now you know the basics of cryptography! It helps keep our information safe in a world where many people use the internet every day.
