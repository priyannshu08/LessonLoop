# Lesson: Understanding RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) ## What is RAG? RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It is a technique used in artificial intelligence (AI) to help computers answer questions better. Imagine you have a friend who is very smart but forgets things easily. Sometimes, they need to look up information in a book or online to give you the best answer. RAG works similarly. It combines two abilities: 1. **Retrieval**: Finding the right information from a large set of data. 2. **Generation**: Creating a new response based on the information found. ### Why Does RAG Matter?
